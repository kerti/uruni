# CONTEXT — Uruni domain language

The shared vocabulary. Use these exact terms in code, API, and user-facing copy (copy in Bahasa Indonesia; identifiers in English). One word per concept — don't introduce synonyms.

Where a concept's Indonesian name is the one the treasurer sees, the identifier is given here in `code font` alongside it. The identifier is what goes in the schema, the API and the code; the Indonesian is a UI label only ([ADR-014](./docs/ADR/014-localization-indonesian-first.md)).

## Core

- **Fund** (kas) — a community's shared pool of money. One instance may hold one or more; one is enough for the validated user. Has a name, a currency (IDR) and a **time zone** (`time_zone`; *zona waktu* in copy): WIB, WITA or WIT, stored as the IANA name (`Asia/Jakarta`, `Asia/Makassar`, `Asia/Jayapura`). It is a fact about the community, not the server or the phone, and decides when a day and a month turn over for the fund ([ADR-041](./docs/ADR/041-the-funds-time-zone.md)). One word: not "timezone", not "wilayah waktu".
- **Account / Location** — *where money physically sits*: Cash (Tunai) or Bank — a fund may have several accounts of either kind, named by the treasurer. Balances are tracked per location because that split is where discrepancies come from.
- **Purpose tag** (pos, `purpose`; *peruntukan* until #385) — what a transaction is *for*, over one pooled balance: **Kas Utama** (routine, `main`), a named **Incidental** (`incidental`), or **Pass-through** (`pass_through`). Exactly one Kas Utama per fund.
- **User** (`user`) — the treasurer's login: an email and an argon2id password hash. One per instance, created once at first run ([ADR-030](./docs/ADR/030-multi-fund-scoping.md)). Distinct from **Member** (a person in the group, who never logs in) and from **Account** (a place money sits). The only entity in the schema that is not scoped to a fund.
- **Member** (anggota) — a person in the group. Name + role/tier only; no email/phone (data minimization). The tier is a history, not a field of the member: see **Member tier**.
- **Member tier** (golongan anggota, `member_tier`) — which tier (golongan) a member is in, month by month. A row is *effective-dated*: it starts at a month (*Mulai bulan*, `effective_from`) and holds until the next row's month, with no end date, so a gap or an overlap cannot be expressed. A past month is priced at the tier the member had then, never today's, so a promotion in June leaves January as it was. A row with no tier means no dues obligation from that month (*Tanpa golongan*), never "unpaid". The current tier is derived from the rows, not stored on the member. Not "membership": `member` with `joined_on` and `inactive_on` already says who belongs to the fund ([ADR-040](./docs/ADR/040-effective-dated-tiers-and-rate-corrections.md)).
- **Dues rate** (tarif iuran) — the recurring amount owed, which **varies by member tier** (golongan; e.g. pelaksana, fungsional pertama/muda/madya). Effective-dated by month like a member's tier: a new rate holds from its month until the next one, and a mistake in one is a **correction**, never an overwrite.
- **Arrears** (tunggakan) — dues owed for periods **before the current one**. Distinct from the current period's own state (`Belum bayar` / `Bayar sebagian` / `Lunas` / `Lunas — sudah bayar di muka`, PRD §7.3), which is read one month at a time; arrears accumulate and are counted in whole months. The two vocabularies never share a word ([ADR-032](./docs/ADR/032-two-level-navigation.md)).

## Movements

- **Transaction** — an immutable posted entry: income or expense; amount (`int64` rupiah); date; location; purpose tag; optional member link, note, receipt photo. **Never edited or deleted** — corrections are new **adjusting entries**.
- **Reimbursement** (talangan, `reimbursement`) — a member fronted money; it becomes owed to them and is settled when repaid. Receipt optional, never required.
- **Incidental collection** (amplop, `incidental`) — a one-off pool for an occasion (sickness, death, sunatan, pension): contributions in, a disbursement out, and a **leftover** that rolls into Kas Utama. The Indonesian word is **amplop**, the envelope the metaphor is built on and the word every screen already used ("Buka amplop", "Tutup amplop"); *kegiatan insidental* is the identifier translated back into Indonesian, not a word the treasurer says, and is not used in copy.
- **Contribution** — money put into an envelope: an ordinary incoming transaction tagged to the envelope, which **may name the member** who gave it (#211). Naming is optional — a guest or an anonymous giver stays an unnamed contribution. Only a contribution or a dues payment carries a member. Indonesian: **sumbangan** (*menyumbang*) — never *iuran*, which is dues.
- **Minimum per member** (sumbangan minimal per anggota, `minimum_per_member`) — an optional figure on an envelope that every expected member is asked to give; one amount for everyone, editable like the occasion, never a posted fact. Not a dues rate: no tiers, no effective dates.
- **Participation** — the per-envelope view of who has contributed and how much, **derived from the ledger** against the envelope's expectation, never stored. The envelope's counterpart of dues status: the ledger says who paid, the envelope (or the dues rates) says who was expected. **Expected** means every member active on the day the envelope opened, tier or no tier; anyone who contributes without being expected still appears, with their amount, but is never shown as "not yet". **Recipients** — the members an envelope is *for* (the sick, the bereaved family, the birthday pair) — are never expected; an envelope names zero or more, always members, editable like the occasion. States: *Sudah menyumbang*, *Belum menyumbang*, *Kurang dari minimal* — deliberately not dues' *Belum bayar* ([ADR-034](./docs/ADR/034-envelope-participation.md)).
- **Pass-through** (titipan) — money collected on behalf of a parent body (e.g. **Kas Bidang**) and forwarded. A purpose tag, so the report can group it; it does **not** come out of any balance, because while it sits in the wallet it really is in the wallet (revised 2026-08-12, [ADR-024](./docs/ADR/024-schema-conventions.md)).
- **Transfer** — the pair of transactions behind a value-neutral movement: between locations (cash deposited at the bank; *Pindah lokasi*, `between_accounts`) or between purposes (`reclass_purpose`) - an incidental's leftover rolled into Kas Utama (`reason = 'roll'`), money the treasurer moves on purpose (*Pindah pos*, `reason = 'allocation'`, ADR-036), or a purpose correction (below). Equal amounts, opposite directions, one `transfer` row binding them, fund total unchanged. Not a synonym for "movement" or "reclassification" — this is the word.
- **Reversal** (pembatalan, `reverses_transaction_id`; the treasurer's verb is *batalkan*) — the new entry that undoes a posted `dues` or `normal` transaction: one linked `adjustment` row going the other way, with the **original**'s amount, location, member, period and effective pos, once only. The original keeps its row and reads *Sudah dibatalkan*. It is dated the original's own day, so it nets in the same month; unless a *cek kas* has counted the original since, and then it is dated **today** in the fund's time zone, because a count freezes every location balance it counted and a reversal dated back before it would change one under a frozen snapshot. The caller types no date. An opening balance, a reimbursement payout, a transfer leg, an adjustment (a *cek kas* plug among them), an entry a *cek kas* line names as its fix, and a reversal cannot be reversed. A reversal is an **adjusting entry** (the genus, with a purpose correction's transfer pair and a *cek kas* plug), and never a **correction**. Not "cancel", "void" or "undo" in code or prose ([ADR-042](./docs/ADR/042-reversing-any-ordinary-transaction.md)).
- **Purpose correction** (perbaikan pos, `reclass_purpose` with `corrects_transaction_id` set) — moving a posted transaction's **pos** to the tag it should always have had. A transfer pair on the original's own account and date, so no balance in any month moves; only the attribution does. The treasurer never *reclassifies* — `reclass_purpose` is the identifier and stays in the engine; she corrects a row's pos ([#267](https://github.com/kerti/uruni/issues/267)).
- **Correction** (perbaikan, `correction`) — setting a wrong fact right without erasing it, in two shapes. A **purpose correction** (above) re-tags a posted row's pos. A **rate** or **member tier** correction states a month again: the new row takes the next `correction` number within that month, the highest number stands and the earlier rows stay behind in the data; it reprices every month the row covers (*Perbaiki nominal*; *Hapus tarif* appends a deleting correction, `deleted`). Neither moves a balance: a correction changes what is *owed* or how a row is *attributed*, never what was *paid*. A **reversal** is never called a correction, and a wrong amount is neither: it is a reversal and a new posting. Only where rule 3 is quoted ("corrections are new adjusting entries") is "correction" the loose word for any fix ([ADR-040](./docs/ADR/040-effective-dated-tiers-and-rate-corrections.md), [ADR-033](./docs/ADR/033-correcting-a-posted-purpose.md)).

## Trust core

- **Balance** — derived by **summing the integer ledger**, never stored as a float.
- **Reconciliation** (cek kas) — comparing the recorded balance to the *actual* cash + bank, per location. States: **cocok** (matches — reconciled) and **selisih** (a difference — shown calmly in terracotta, never alarm-red).
- **Reconciliation snapshot** — a saved point-in-time record of expected vs. actual and how any difference was resolved.

## Sharing

- **Public report** (laporan publik) — an unauthenticated, filterable, read-only page at a stable unguessable link the treasurer shares once. One month at a time; shows everything the PRD accepts making public (names, amounts, payment status), with filters. Not a member portal (no accounts). The link changes only when the treasurer deliberately makes a new one ([ADR-035](./docs/ADR/035-public-report.md)).
- **Backup / export** — one full-data backup file (canonical, restorable: the ledger as JSON plus every receipt photo) + optional **Excel**; automatic daily server-side backups, restorable in the app. No emailed backups.
