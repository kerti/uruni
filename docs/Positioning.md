# Uruni — Positioning

> **Uruni**
> *Kelola dana kebersamaan.*
> The calm way to keep your community's shared money — and know it always adds up.

---

## Who it's for

The **accidental treasurer** — the person who got told *"Bu, jadi bendahara ya,"* didn't ask for it, and now looks after a small group's shared money. Validated with a real one: an office-unit treasurer, 8 people, ~Rp 1–2 million a month, running it all off a Google Sheet on her laptop and wishing she could just do it from her phone.

## The core insight (from the interview)

Her hardest task isn't chasing people — nagging is fine in a small group. It's **making the recorded balance match the real money.** The cash lives in two places (her wallet and a personal bank account mixed with her salary), the routine kas and the incidental funds get lumped together, and someone always takes cash to shop and forgets to write it down. So the numbers drift, and she has to hunt down the difference.

She doesn't fear being *suspected* — everyone can already see the sheet. What she carries is a quiet **moral weight** of holding shared money, and her peace of mind comes from one thing:

> **Tenang, karena catatan selalu cocok dengan uang yang ada.**
> *Calm, because the records always match the money.*

Her wish, in her own words: *"tidak usah berpikir"* — to not have to think about it.

## What Uruni is

A **mobile app** that makes the treasurer's job effortless capture and honest math:

- **One-tap transaction capture, anywhere** — the thing a spreadsheet can't do from a phone at a market stall. This is the wedge.
- **A running balance that's always honest**, with a simple **reconciliation check**: does what's recorded match the cash in the wallet and the money in the account? Flag the gap, don't make her hunt for it.
- **Two money locations tracked** (cash vs. bank), because that split is where her discrepancies come from.
- **One pooled balance, with purpose tags** (kas utama, incidental collections, pass-through to kas bidang) — so funds stay separated *in meaning* without her juggling separate pots, and leftover incidental money folds back in cleanly.
- **Per-member dues that vary by person** (by rank/role), and simple "who's paid" tracking — without turning into a nagging machine.
- **Reimbursements in seconds, receipts optional** — because nobody wants a receipt for Rp 2.000 of parking.
- **Records that can't be lost** — the source of truth lives on an always-on server, so a lost or broken phone never loses anything. This reliability is something she named unprompted.
- **An optional, on-demand ledger export** for anyone who wants the full picture. Kept out of responsibility, not because members read it daily — they don't.

## How it treats data

**Uruni the project holds nothing.** There is no Uruni-run service sitting on communities' money records. Instead, a community runs its **own always-on Uruni server** — one source of truth in the cloud, so records are safe even if a phone is lost, and there's never a "which copy is right?" question.

- The server is **open source and self-hostable**. A community's data lives on the community's *own* instance, under its own control.
- For the validated user, a technical helper stands the instance up. It's not a tap-to-install consumer app, and we don't pretend it is.
- **AGPL-3.0**: anyone who hosts Uruni for their community must keep the code open. The trust story is auditable and true by construction.

Every competitor is a company's server holding your data. With Uruni, the server is *yours*.

## What Uruni is deliberately NOT

Resident databases · digital voting · complaint handling · inventory · QRIS reconciliation · accounting journals · analytics dashboards — and, for the small-group treasurer we've actually validated, **not a payment-chasing / reminder machine** either. Chasing wasn't the pain; reconciliation was.

> There are excellent products for those. Uruni exists to help small communities keep their shared fund honest, from a phone, without thinking hard.

## Principles

1. Small groups first.
2. Simpler than a spreadsheet, and reliable enough to replace it.
3. Mobile-first — capture anywhere.
4. The numbers always add up, and Uruni proves it.
5. Records that can't be lost.
6. **Your data stays yours — on your community's own server. Uruni the project holds nothing.**
7. Free, forever. A gift, not a business.
8. No feature creep — ever.
