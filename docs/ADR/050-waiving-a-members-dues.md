# ADR-050 - Waiving a member's dues

**Status:** `draft` · M11 ([#763](https://github.com/kerti/uruni/issues/763)) · grilled with the maintainer 2026-11-04 · [ADR index](./README.md)

**Context.** A community sometimes decides that a member owes nothing for a while - after their house burned down, a robbery, a death in the family. Uruni could not say so. The only workaround was a Rp0 kategori from the first waived month, and the dues code classifies owed 0 / paid 0 as *Lunas*, so Status iuran, the twelve-month grid (ADR-044) and the public report would tell the whole community the member paid months nobody paid. It also recorded no decision, took two backdated kategori changes per waiver, and was not something a treasurer would find. Talangan already has the idea: a claim can be *Putihkan* - real, kept in the history, no longer owed, undoable (PRD 7.4, ADR-024). Dues had nothing like it.

It passes the test every new concept here must (the real need: real, distinct, in scope): the need is the maintainer's community's, the workaround states something false on the public report, and honest dues status is PRD 7.3's job; it chases nobody (PRD section 4).

**Decision.**

**A waiver** (pemutihan) is the group's decision that a member's dues for a month stop being owed. The treasurer's verb is **Putihkan**; a waived month reads **Diputihkan** - talangan's words, because it is the same idea: an amount really owed that stops being owed, kept, undoable. A waiver moves no money and posts no ledger row, so balances and reconciliation never depend on it.

- **Which months:** any month inside the member's active window (joined_on through the month holding inactive_on) that is still owed - arrears, the current month, or up to twelve months ahead. Whole months, the whole remainder; "pays less from now on" is a cheaper kategori, not a waiver.
- **A partly paid month** can be waived: the waiver covers what is left, and the payment stays paid, in the ledger and in Riwayat. A fully paid month (Lunas, or paid ahead) cannot be - nothing is owed, and giving money back is a refund, a real *keluar*. If a payment on a waived month is later reversed as a mistake, the month stays waived. Rejected: refusing partly paid months, which would make the treasurer reverse money that really came in.
- **One state at a time:** a waived month is not owed, so it is not offered on the payment form, and the ledger refuses a dues payment for it by name. To take money for it anyway, un-waive first.
- **Storage:** one row per waived member-month (member, month, note, when recorded), unique per member and month, in the one schema file (ADR-025), `STRICT` with CHECKs. Eligibility (inside the window, still owed) needs the dues computation, so the ledger enforces it. Several months waived at once each carry the shared note; there is no binding row, since a waiver has no sum. Rejected: a date range in one row, which would have to split the moment one month is un-waived. Undo deletes the month's row, as un-waiving a talangan clears `waived_on`; a waiver is not a ledger row, so immutability (rule 3) does not reach it.
- **The note** is optional (*Catatan (opsional)*) and the treasurer's only: shown in the app, kept in backups, **never on the public report or its PDF**. Someone's misfortune, broadcast by a link anyone can forward, fails on dignity; the group knows why it decided.
- **Where:** one door, the member's card on Status iuran. A card that owes gets **Putihkan** beside *Catat iuran*; it opens *Putihkan iuran*, a one-record dialog: the member (Anggota, Kategori), then the months - the payment form's checklist of what is still owed, from the same endpoint (so the two can never disagree), each with its remaining amount (not editable), *Pilih semua tunggakan*, and *Tambah <bulan>* for months ahead - a read-only *Total diputihkan*, the note, then Tutup | Putihkan. A waived card offers **Batalkan pemutihan** (a confirm dialog, ADR-046) in their place. Not in Catat, which records money; not in Riwayat > Iuran, which is the payment history.
- **How it reads:** a fifth status, *Diputihkan*, in neutral ink - not green (no money came in), not terracotta (nothing is left to do) - wherever a month's status shows: Status iuran, the twelve-month grid with a fifth shape in its legend, the public report's dues list and its *Status iuran* filter, and the PDF. The grid keeps its fill series (hollow, half, filled, dash); the new shape is picked from a candidate sheet rendered in context. A waived month drops out of the arrears count and the Tunggakan chip. It ends a *Sudah dibayar sampai* chain: Lunas, Diputihkan, Lunas reads paid through the first month, because nobody paid the second.
- **Edges:** a rate or kategori change leaves a waived month waived; a waiver outside the member's window (after a later deactivation) has no effect and returns if they are reactivated. Backups' canonical JSON carries waivers and restore reads them (ADR-012) - in format 3, which is still unreleased when this lands (the last release, v0.11.0-alpha.5, writes format 2), so the new array rides it without a bump, and a format-3 file without it restores with none; if the parked Excel export ([#328](https://github.com/kerti/uruni/issues/328)) is ever built, it gets a *Pemutihan iuran* sheet; the demo seed waives one member. Nothing totals waivers over time - no "Rp X diputihkan tahun ini", the analytics PRD section 4 rules out.

**Consequences.**

- A new table and a new status on the public report: hard to take back once communities use it.
- Every surface that shows a dues status gains one state, and the twelve-month grid a fifth shape.
- The dues computation (owed, arrears, paid-through, the outstanding months) reads waivers; the payment path refuses waived months.
- The Rp0-kategori workaround stays possible but is no longer the way; nothing migrates, since nobody's data uses it as a waiver yet.
