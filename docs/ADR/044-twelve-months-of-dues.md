# ADR-044 - Twelve months of dues, in the app and on the report

**Status:** Accepted · implemented in the Production milestone ([#619](https://github.com/kerti/uruni/issues/619): [#684](https://github.com/kerti/uruni/pull/684) the ledger and route, [#685](https://github.com/kerti/uruni/pull/685) the app view, then the report and PDF) - drafted and grilled 2026-10-23, the report scope set by the maintainer the same day · change only by adding a superseding ADR · supersedes [ADR-035](./035-public-report.md) on two points, named at the foot · reads [ADR-032](./032-two-level-navigation.md), [ADR-037](./037-report-shows-the-month-as-it-ended.md), [ADR-040](./040-effective-dated-tiers-and-rate-corrections.md) and [ADR-041](./041-the-funds-time-zone.md) without superseding them · [ADR index](./README.md)

**Context.** The treasurer checks dues compliance one month at a time: the dues status screen (`/dues`, opened from the top of Riwayat's Iuran tab) and the report each show a single period, straight from `DuesStatusForPeriod`. To see whether a member has kept up over the year she opens twelve months, one after another. #619 asked for one table instead: each member's status across the recent months.

ADR-035 section 3 kept anything arrears-shaped off the public page - *"A running 'Tunggakan N bulan' on a public page reads as a debtor list - close enough to nagging (PRD section 4, section 7.5) to stay in the app"* - and said *"the month is the unit; there is no paging and no all-time view."* A twelve-month grid on the report is that debtor list made legible: a row of empty cells says "Tunggakan 8 bulan" without the words. #619 was therefore first scoped as app-only.

**The maintainer overruled that on 2026-10-23: the grid goes on the report and its PDF too.** The reasoning this ADR records: every cell the grid shows is already public - any neighbour can open each of the twelve months and read the same status in the same words. PRD section 7.9 already accepted that "the public URL exposes member names and payment status" as the treasurer's transparency intent. The grid removes eleven clicks; it does not publish anything new. What PRD section 4 rules out is *chasing* - reminders, automated nagging - and a page nobody is sent to, which says nothing to anyone, is not that. The line this ADR keeps is the one between showing status and pressing on it (below).

PRD-wise this was a scope call against section 4 and 7.9, made by the maintainer; PRD section 7.3 and 7.9, their PRD-ID.md twins and CONTEXT.md change in the PR that ships the code (Consequences).

## Decision

**One grid, the same reading in three places: the dues status screen in the app, the report page, and the report PDF.** One row per member, one cell per month, at most twelve months.

**The twelve months.** The twelve months ending at an *end month*, oldest on the left:
- In the app, the end month is the fund's current month, read on the server in the fund's time zone ([ADR-041](./041-the-funds-time-zone.md)); the client sends none.
- On the **report and its PDF**, it is the selected month - so a September page shows October of last year through September, and a printed September statement never carries months after its title.
- Leading months in which nobody has an obligation are not drawn: the grid starts at the first of the twelve in which some member owes. A member's `joined_on` defaults to the setup day (PRD 7.1), so a fund set up four months ago has four columns, and a join date backdated for live arrears widens it back to where that member's dues begin. There is no fund-level adoption date to clip at, and clipping at the fund's first transaction would hide the very months live arrears open. The twelve months never reach past the current month.
- A member with no obligation in any of the twelve has no row.

**A cell** is that member's status for that month, as `DuesStatusForPeriod` reports it today, drawn as three states and the absence of an obligation.
- `Lunas`, `Bayar sebagian`, `Belum bayar`. A month `DuesStatusForPeriod` reads as `Lunas - sudah bayar di muka` is drawn as `Lunas`: in a row of months, paying ahead is already visible as the paid cells to its right, and a separate mark would land only on the month before a gap (the one-month displays already show a plain `Lunas` whenever a "paid through" month exists, and `dues=paid` already matches both).
- **No obligation** - a month for which `DuesStatusForPeriod` returns no row for the member, for whatever reason: before the month of `joined_on`; after the month of `inactive_on` (a member owes through the month they went inactive, in full); no tier in force that month (*Tanpa golongan*); or a tier with no rate in force yet (*Tarif belum ditentukan*). It is shown neutral and empty, never as `Belum bayar`, and never says which reason. It is not a status the ledger owes anyone; it is the absence of a due. A zero rate is not one of these: `DuesStatusForPeriod` reads a free tier as `Lunas` (owed Rp 0), and the grid says what the table says. A payment recorded against a month with no obligation is not shown, as in the one-month table; it counts in the balance as always.

Status is **as of today**, the reading ADR-037 kept for Iuran: a member who paid March late shows `Lunas` on March, on every print made since. Tiers and rates are the effective-dated ones ([ADR-040](./040-effective-dated-tiers-and-rate-corrections.md)): a later change of tier leaves a past cell where it was. A past cell does move when something it rests on is recorded or restated - a late payment or a reversal, a rate or tier correction (ADR-040 accepts that a correction reprices the past at any age), or a join or leave date moved, which shifts whole months in or out of no obligation.

**Encoding.** Each status has its own **shape** as well as its colour - filled, half-filled, hollow, a dash for no obligation - so the grid reads in a black-and-white print of the PDF and for anyone who doesn't see the colours. The tones are the one-month badges' own, the Design-System's gentle semantic ones: green family for `Lunas`, terracotta for `Bayar sebagian`, neutral for `Belum bayar` and for no obligation; never alarm-red. Shapes are drawn, never typed as glyphs: the PDF's embedded font subset has no geometric shapes, and the report's CSP allows styles only from its one nonce'd `<style>` block, so no inline style attributes. Every cell carries its month and status as text for assistive technology (a visually hidden label on the page, the legend on the PDF). A legend sits under the grid in all three places. Column heads are short month names - an exception to the Design-System's rule that a month is never abbreviated, written there in the PR that ships this - and a year change is marked on the first column of each year.

**What the grid never does** - this is the line between showing and pressing:
- **No count.** No "Tunggakan N bulan", no total of unpaid months, no per-member summary column, on the report or the PDF. (The app's roster keeps its arrears badge, ADR-032 - the treasurer's private view - but the grid adds none.)
- **No ranking.** Rows are by name, as every member list reads, never sorted or grouped by how much is unpaid, and no filter selects members by their grid.
- **No amounts.** Owed and paid figures stay in the one-month table; the grid is status only.

**On the report**, the grid opens the Iuran section, above its status filter and the selected month's table, which stays as it is. It is not drawn when fewer than two months owe anything - a one-column grid repeats the table. On a past month it carries its own "per <today>" caption, since it is read as of today while the header above it is as of the month's end (Consequences). Filters:
- `member=`, `purpose=` and `dir=` filter the transactions only, as they do today. None touches the grid, as none touches the dues table.
- `dues=` filters the table by the selected month's status, as now, and does not touch the grid either. A grid of only the members unpaid this month, each with a year behind them, is the debtor list this ADR keeps from forming, and "no filter selects members by their grid" has to hold for the last column too. The grid is every member the twelve months owe, filtered or not, and the PDF - which carries no filters - draws the same one.

It is server-rendered and works without JavaScript; on a phone it fits the column by being dots, not by scrolling.

**In the app**, the grid is a second view of the existing dues status screen, not a new screen or setting. ADR-032 keeps the Iuran tab to the payment history and puts the monthly reading on this screen, and a year stacked onto the tab is the clutter #228 removed. The switch between the one-month list and the grid is part of the screen; the month field and the "belum bayar" toggle belong to the list. Cells are not controls (twelve cannot be 44px wide on a phone, the Design-System's floor); acting stays where it is - the list's member cards, with their payment history and reversal, and Catat pembayaran, whose form already lists the months a member still owes.

**One derivation.** The ledger computes the grid in one call - members, tiers, rates and payments read once for all twelve months, a fixed handful of reads however many members or months (the #532 discipline) - not as twelve calls to `DuesStatusForPeriod`. The reason is the report, not the roster's size: it is unauthenticated, recomputed on every request, and queues behind the treasurer's writes on the one SQLite connection (ADR-035), so twelve calls would multiply every hit's reads by twelve; and one pass reads one consistent ledger state. It reuses the rules `DuesStatusForPeriod` already owns (`memberOwesPeriod`, `pricing.priceAt`, `classifyDuesStatus`, the reversed-row exclusion) and adds none; it has no use for `paidThrough` or the paid-in-advance upgrade, since both draw as `Lunas`. Its result is the same, cell by cell, as twelve such calls, and a test pins that over a fixture that hits every rule - a reversal, a prepaid run, a rate raise, a tier change, the join and leave months, no tier, no rate - as #532 pinned the batched roster against the one-at-a-time one.

## Consequences

- The report gains one bounded section: at most twelve cells per member. ADR-035's "no paging" still holds - the twelve months are fixed, not paged.
- The grid is a field of the report's one assembly, so the page and the PDF draw the same cells and nothing is computed in a template (ADR-035). The app reads the same ledger call through one new authenticated route, the only new route.
- A neighbour can read a member's year at a glance. That is the intent, and the cost the maintainer accepted; the guard against it becoming a debtor list is the "never does" list above, not a narrower page. The pattern of no-obligation cells also shows the months a member joined or left, which `ledger.Report`'s comment says the report omits; paging the one-month tables already shows it, and the comment is reworded with the code.
- A past month's PDF mixes two as-of dates. ADR-037 states every figure as of the selected month's end except Iuran, which stays as of today; a September statement printed in December carries a header "per 30 September" and a grid read in December. The carve-out was one month wide and is now twelve.
- The PDF's Iuran page already starts on its own page, and a roster of twenty fills it, so the grid goes onto a second page: rows never split, the column heads repeat as the PDF's other tables' do, and the legend stays with the grid. On a 390px phone, twelve columns leave roughly 20px each once the name has its share, which the month heads must be checked against on a device.
- No schema change and no backup-format change: the grid is derived from members, tiers, rates and the ledger.
- Tests beyond the pin above: the grid's reads do not grow with the fund (as #532's), and a journey for the app view and one for the report grid, each with its `COVERAGE.md` row ([ADR-039](./039-e2e-every-operation-gated-in-ci.md)).
- **Copy** is proposed to the maintainer before it lands, after a sweep of CONTEXT.md, PRD-ID and `copy/id.ts`: the grid's heading, the legend's word for no obligation, the short month heads, the screen-reader label and anything that says what the grid is as of. The four status words are the existing ones.
- **Docs, in the PR that ships the code:** PRD 7.3 (the status screen also reads twelve months) and 7.9 ("one month at a time" and "its dues status (no arrears count)"), their PRD-ID.md twins, CONTEXT.md (**Public report** says "One month at a time"; the grid wants its one word), the Design-System's month-name exception, ADR-035's status line and the ADR index row. `Decisions.md` is the orchestrator's.

## Scope check (PRD section 4)

Beyond the maintainer's call that the grid goes on the report: no new screen (a second view of the status screen), no setting, no dependency (fpdf already draws; no new font), no schema or backup change. New: one read-only ledger call, one authenticated route, one report section with its PDF drawing, and one word for the treasurer to learn (the grid). None of the non-goals is approached - no reminder, count, ranking or amount - and the Design-System's "no ledgers-as-grids aesthetic" is held by what the grid leaves out: figures, totals and anything red.

**Superseded in ADR-035:** in section 3, the reason given for keeping arrears-shaped information off the public page ("reads as a debtor list - close enough to nagging ... to stay in the app"), to the extent of a twelve-month status grid; "No arrears count" itself stands, and the grid adds none. And, in the month-is-the-unit paragraph, "no all-time view", to the extent that the Iuran section now looks back up to twelve months from the selected one; the month is still the unit everywhere else, and there is still no paging.
